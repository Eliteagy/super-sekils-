# 01 - Interview Process at Atlassian

**Tags:** #atlassian #interview #career #hiring

## Overview
The hiring process at Atlassian (circa 8 years ago) consisted of multiple stages testing both technical depth and cultural fit.

## The Process
1. **HackerRank Coding Quiz:** Aced with full marks.
2. **First Technical Interview:**
   - Interviewers provided a Cloudflare whitepaper on custom domains.
   - 10 minutes of isolated reading, followed by a Q&A to articulate the paper's contents.
   - General architectural questions regarding microservices, containers, etc.
3. **Second Technical Interview:**
   - A real-world troubleshooting exercise (application problem leading to a denial of service).
   - Required prompting the interviewer for information to resolve the incident.
   - A question on latency-based DNS routing (the candidate reasoned from first principles, suggesting triangulation, though the real answer likely involves geolocation databases).
4. **Values Interview:**
   - Asked the interviewer: *"Looking back 12 months from now, what would I have had to achieve for you to say it was a good decision hiring me?"*
   - **The Answer:** Build a self-service load balancing application for the internal Atlassian platform, similar to AWS ALBs but for internal developers. The candidate confidently proposed building it using Python.
